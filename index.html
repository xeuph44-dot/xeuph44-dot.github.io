01 — My Story

My name is Helena Zaruba, and I am a fourth-year student of Multimedia Fine Arts at the Faculty of Fine Arts of the University of Porto. Several of my video projects focus on the consequences of war and its impact on everyday life. In photography, I am particularly interested in subjects connected to my own life and environment. On one of my photographic series documents the people I worked with while employed in a restaurant, focusing on everyday relationships, work routines, and human connections. Alongside photography and video, I also practice traditional tile painting outside the academic environment. I create hand-painted ceramic tiles using cobalt blue pigments and historical decorative patterns inspired by traditional ceramic techniques.

02 — Method

My artistic practice is primarily focused on photography and video. I am interested in documenting real experiences, personal stories, and contemporary social issues that shape people’s lives. Through my work, I explore themes such as war, memory, identity, friendship, migration, and social change.

03 — Values

Through documentary filmmaking, I seek to give space to real voices and experiences, including stories of people close to me whose lives have changed significantly in recent years. Across different media, I am interested in connecting personal experiences with broader social realities and creating work that is both personal and socially engaged.
